Galho

Nagaland

The Heritage

A foundational dish of the Angami tribe in Nagaland, Galho is a deeply comforting, soupy concoction of rice, seasonal greens, and often smoked meats. It is not merely sustenance, but a reflection of the region's agrarian rhythms and foraging traditions. Variations exist from village to village, but its soul remains tied to the communal hearth and the distinctive flavors of fermented bamboo shoot and indigenous herbs.

REGION

Kohima, Nagaland

VESSEL

Traditional Clay Pot

PREP TIME

45 Minutes

INGREDIENTS

Rice Smoked Pork Axone Mustard Leaves
